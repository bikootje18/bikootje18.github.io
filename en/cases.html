500,000 products labeled and assembled every month

Situation

A consumer products brand asked whether we could label and assemble 500,000 products per month for the retail market. A structural assignment, high volumes, strict requirements.

What we do

We structured our entire process around this partnership. After an initial setup phase, we now operate in fixed fortnightly cycles: goods are delivered, processed and collected. The collaboration has since expanded — alongside labeling and assembly, we now also build displays for them.

Daily B2B fulfillment for a party supplies wholesaler

Situation

A party supplies wholesaler needed a reliable fulfillment partner for the structural processing of their B2B flow: multiple incoming containers per week and daily outbound orders for their customers.

What we do

We receive multiple containers weekly, store the goods and pick orders daily at item level. The picked orders are assembled by us into boxes or pallets, ready for collection by their customers.

✓ 8-year fulfillment partnership

✓ Daily execution without disruption

Full operational deployment at the client's site

Situation

A company needed a partner who could not just take on work, but run the complete operation at their own location. Not a staffing agency — a partner that takes full responsibility for the entire operation.

What we do

We place a team of 30 to 50 of our own staff at the client's site and handle all operational activities there. The team scales flexibly up and down with demand — without the client having to deal with planning, staffing or supervision.

✓ Operational on-site for nearly a year

✓ Flexible up and down scaling

✓ Fully managed
