What we do

Your hub in the European supply chain

Service Pack operates as a central hub for businesses that need an efficient intermediate stop in their logistics chain. We handle cross-docking, consolidation of shipments and distribution to distribution centres or end customers.

Our location in Etten-Leur — 30 minutes from the ports of Rotterdam and Antwerp — makes us ideally positioned for European operations. Combined with our value-added services such as labeling, repacking and rework, we offer a complete solution from one location.
